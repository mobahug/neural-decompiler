## What the result supports

- **Causal evidence for one decoded variable at one stage.** This is the project's first preregistered,
  outcome-bearing intervention test of a decoded variable whose necessity-, sufficiency- and control-oriented families
  all passed: the read coordinate `r(x) = ⟨x, w_c⟩` of `RESID_PRE.L3` at the cue position carries essentially the whole
  one/two shift of the singular–plural contrast, and an equal-length edit along the cue's own off-read change carries
  essentially none. The four earlier routes (005, 006, 007, 025) never reached this.
- **The read part is a small slice of the cue change.** It is about 14 % (8.6–18.9 %) of the cue change's centred
  squared length at `p_c`; the remaining ~86 % has close to zero net effect through all readers combined
  (`F_swap_pl` 0.044 vs `F_rem` −0.009). That is an empirical finding, not a consequence of the definition.
- **Through `L03.H04`, predominantly but not uniquely** (secondary, descriptive): the head-only arms carry about 88 %
  of the removal and 89 % of the installation (removal share 0.882, installation share 0.894; value-only 0.877 and
  0.892, so the head's attention change at `p_c` contributes almost nothing; the effect runs through the value at
  `p_c`). About 11–12 % reaches the logits through other readers of `x(p_c)` (other layer-3 heads, MLP3, `L05.H07`,
  block 5's path), which the design does not decompose.
- **The program's predictions held under artificial edits** (P, 33/36): the frozen chain-plus-readout predicted the
  removal and installation fractions within the calibrated tolerances before any target prompt ran. By design this is a
  check of the program's fidelity (with exact upstream inputs it is close to a recomputation; design §5), in mean
  fractions, where its per-noun errors largely cancel (below).
- **What it does not change by itself:** C002 and C003 stay as registered. Design §1: "A claim scoped to that stage
  could then be reviewed for higher maturity. That review is a separate decision."

## Secondary results (no label force)

- **The stratum.** `three` (which did not enter `d̂_T`) behaves like `two` on every statistic: R 36/36, I 36/36,
  `A_amp` < 0 28/36, `A_neg` > 0 36/36. `many` shows R and I in 36/36 but weaker (`F_rem` median 0.21, N 0.77; 15 % of
  its shift does not pass through `p_c`), and `A_amp` < 0 in only 23/36: **under the primary rules `many` would read
  `…DIRECTION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`.** The stratum cannot change the label either way.
- **D is the narrowest family and is saturation-limited:** `A_amp` passed by 3 frames; the head's transported signal
  nearly doubles under NEC-AMP (ΔT ×1.94) while `Δc` moves only −0.136 nats.
- **The isotropic controls:** the read edits beat the median of 8 random directions in 36/36 frames for removal and for
  installation.
- **Doses and swaps:** the half doses give 0.70 and 0.72 (non-linear); the whole-position swap gives 1.008 (installation)
  and 0.044 (removal); additivity `F_rem + F_ins` 0.989.
- **The preregistered head counts** (`F_head_rem` < 1, `F_head_ins` > 0; 36/36 each) are nearly vacuous; the shares
  above are the informative statistic.

## Simpler or competing explanations (the confirmation review, § 6.3)

| explanation | status |
|---|---|
| generic disruption of the cue's information | excluded: D's signs (28/36, 36/36) and the inert equal-length off-read and isotropic edits |
| the read edit is "louder" after LayerNorm | excluded: equal centred lengths; post-LN sizes comparable (‖γ₃⊙e‖/σ 7.44 read vs 7.95 off-read vs 7.32 isotropic) |
| a subspace-patching or dormant-direction illusion | largely excluded by the complementary arms: the read part alone reproduces the whole-position swap, and the full natural remainder alone does nothing |
| off-distribution edited states | partly addressed, not excluded: swaps move `r` only to the other prompt's natural value, but the composite states are novel; nothing tests whether they lie on the data manifold |
| other readers of `x(p_c)` | within the primary scope by design; quantified by the head arms (~11–12 % outside `L03.H04`), not decomposed |
| saturation | explains D's weak `A_amp`; does not threaten R or I |
| `w_c` estimated in-sample from one/two | not excluded for the primary claim (new frames, same pair); the stratum shows transfer to `three` and partial transfer to `many` |
| R and I follow partly from the definition of `w_c` | true for the head-mediated route; the empirical content is that the head's output at `p_t` is the bottleneck for ~88–89 % of `Δc`, that the off-read remainder is inert through all readers, and that the effect is complete rather than partial |
| site, layer, template or checkpoint specificity | not tested: no site or layer negative control, no cue-final frames, one checkpoint, a one-dimensional manipulation check |
| the inferential population | unstated: the binomial tails treat these 36 frames of one template as exchangeable units; p ≈ 1.5e-11 is a statement about these frames under a sign-symmetric null, not a population rate |
