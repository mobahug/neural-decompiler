## Finalist prior-art audits

### `regular-plural` — `PARTIAL_OVERLAP_WITH_EXPLICIT_GAP` (searched through 2026-09-17)

- Behavior variants: count-cued English noun number selection (one NOUN vs two NOUNS; each NOUN vs several NOUNS; coordinated count context with an intervening adjective), regular plural inflection and spelling allomorphy (-s, -es, consonant-y to -ies), quantifier-noun and numeral-noun number agreement, subject-verb and reflexive number agreement as the adjacent grammatical-number mechanism family, grammatical-number and inflectional features, probes, and steering directions
- Model families: Pythia (14M-6.9B, including pinned Pythia-70M), GPT-2 Small/Medium/XL, Gemma-2B and Gemma-2, BERT, DeBERTa, XLNet, XGLM, OLMo-2, Qwen2.5, Llama-3.1 (inflection probing)
- Explanation levels: component, edge, feature, subspace, program
- Validation modes: causal, reconstruction, self-repair, held-out intervention
- Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02132, https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19647, https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.785/, https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04354, https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.144/, https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.591/, https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.14328
- Explicit gap: Close work maps grammatical number either as a representation (linearly decodable, steerable inflection subspaces across models including Pythia) or as agreement circuits that read a subject's number to choose a verb or reflexive form (SAE feature circuits in Pythia-70M, causal mediation and circuit probing in GPT-2, head/neuron accounts in Gemma-2B, DAS causal variables across Pythia scales). None locates the head/MLP-level mechanism by which an unmodified Pythia-70M reads a numeral or quantifier cue and selects the inflected noun form itself, none predicts held-out multi-component interventions for that behavior prospectively, none reconstructs the behavior from the proposed components, none tests self-repair or backup paths for it, and none quantifies the unexplained residual. The proposed Experiment 005 contribution is therefore a component-level, repair-aware, reconstruction-tested causal account of count-cued noun number selection with prospectively predicted held-out interventions and explicit residual accounting; it is not a novelty claim about grammatical number representations, which are already mapped.

## Limitations

- Compactness is exploratory: it ranks components on discovery cases and tests fixed cumulative sets on validation cases within development data. It establishes neither completeness, minimality, self-repair robustness, nor a human-readable mechanism.
- Every matched pair differs only in its controlling cue, so the cue-shuffle condition coincides with the counterfactual prompt; gate 8 is therefore implied by the contrast measurements rather than independent of them.
- The `dated-event` ordinal template applies the frozen shared number pairs, most of which are not calendar dates.
- Behavioral screening compares the intended inflected form with its matched alternative. For `regular-plural` the local heuristic's plural prediction (`citys`) is never the scored foil (` city`), so gate 9 is vacuous for that candidate and what is screened is count-cued singular/plural selection rather than `es`/`ies` allomorphy; for `ordinal-suffix` the heuristic predicts the actual foil (`st` for `11`), so gate 9 is informative.
- Ordinal manifest properties frozen before the run: the first `bare-numeral` surface variant is a single-token prompt scored at position 0 without a BOS token; development B-numbers are single tokens while most holdout B-numbers tokenize into several pieces, so the development-to-holdout drop gate is confounded with a tokenization shift for that candidate.
- Split membership is predeclared by the literal per-split pools and number pairs; the recorded seed 20260916 selects nothing.
- Only the two retained candidates were screened; `degree-inflection` was eliminated before any output for tokenizer infeasibility.
