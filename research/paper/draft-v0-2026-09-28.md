## Abstract

We ask whether the computation by which a pretrained transformer turns a count cue into the choice between a singular
and a plural noun can be recovered as an explicit, compact, human-readable causal mechanism. The mechanism is to be
specified before it is tested on examples and interventions it was not built from.

**The study.** In pinned Pythia-70M-deduped we ran 21 experiments (005–025). Stages of the mechanism were formulated on
exposed data, frozen as programs, and tested once each, in single preregistered confirmation runs. Not every test
passed, and every failure was kept. The composite account was assembled afterwards, from those runs and from
exploratory work.

**First level: localization.** The singular-versus-plural contrast is localized to three components: the layer-0 MLP
at the cue position, the attention head `L03.H04`, and the layer-4 and layer-5 MLPs.
- For two cue pairs, on development data and nineteen held-out nouns, exact replacement of these components recovers
  about 98 % of the contrast shift, and isolating them retains about 78 %.
- This rests on exploratory and calibration data: its one preregistered confirmation run failed its behavioral
  precondition.

**Second level: stage programs.** They are written from the weights, each frame's measured reference state and
quantities locked from exposed data. By rules frozen before the target prompts were executed, they predicted:
- **for an intervention on the cue's layer-0 encoding:** its consequences stage by stage, from the encoding to the
  head's output. This held for new cues from the tested lexical classes. In new frames it held for most stages, given
  their measured reference states.
- **for real cue swaps:** the contrast change end to end.
  - On a first wiring, later found defective in coordinated frames, it passed by a small margin for new cues and nouns
    in exposed frames, pooled over templates. It had no positive skill in coordinated frames, and in new frames its
    label was negative.
  - With block 0 completed, for new determiner-like, quantity and adjective cues, three conditions passed relative to
    the readout's own ceiling (new frames conditional), and one was an `ENVELOPE_ONLY_FAILURE`. There is no aggregate
    label.

**The answer, attribute by attribute.**
- **Explicit and specified before testing:** largely yes, stage by stage.
- **Compact and human-readable:** in parts only.
- **Tested on interventions it was not built from:** only partly. The stage predictions concern the same intervention
  used in exploration, applied to new cues and frames.
- **Runs without the network:** no. The programs need each frame's measured reference state.
- **Causal:** not established. Four preregistered routes to causal status were designed. Three concerned the localized
  components, and one a decoded direction. Three ran, and none established causal status.

We report prospectively predictive programs whose causal gates remain unestablished, and the protocol that made this
boundary explicit.

## 1. Task and question

**The behavior.** The measured quantity is the contrast `c(x) = log P(singular) − log P(plural)` between a noun's two
forms (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md@eeff9ff:28). A **frame** is a prompt template with a cue
slot and a noun slot. There are three families:
- **cardinal:** a numeral cue directly before the noun;
- **quantifier:** a quantifier cue directly before the noun;
- **coordinated-adjective:** a cue, then an adjective, then the noun.

In the first two families the cue position `p_c` is the prediction position `p_t`. In the third, `p_t = p_c + 1`. The
nouns are single-token regular nouns.

**The cues.**
- The localization (§3) uses the matched cue pairs `one`/`two` and `each`/`several`.
- The later experiments put many other words in the cue slot. Each is compared with the frame's frozen singular
  **reference cue**. The words came from several lexical classes:
  - determiner-like words, numerals, quantity words, possessives or pronouns, and adjectives (011:16–21);
  - determiner-like, quantity and adjective words (023:47);
  - adjectives and nouns (025:99–102).

  The end-to-end quantity is `Δc(f, w, n) = c(frame f, cue w, noun n) − c(frame f, reference cue, noun n)`
  (020:21–24). From 009 to 019 the measured quantities were the E-patch's intermediate changes.
- **Exposed** cues, frames and nouns are those a program was built or calibrated on. **Fresh** or **new** ones had
  never been executed when the program was frozen.

**The question.** The sequence set out to answer: “Can the computation by which an unmodified pretrained transformer
converts an external count/quantifier cue into the selection of a singular versus plural noun form be recovered as an
explicit, compact, human-readable causal mechanism that is specified before, and then tested on, untouched examples and
interventions it was not built from?”
(docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-17-experiment-005-regular-plural-mechanism-design.md:142–146)

**The model.** `EleutherAI/pythia-70m-deduped`, pinned at revision `e93a9faa9c77e5d09219f6c868bfc7a1bd65593c`. It runs
in float32 on CPU (020:28).

**The behavior's origin.** It was chosen by a behavior screen, which was exploratory.

## 2. Prospective methodology

**The protocol.** The experiments shared a protocol, applied with named exceptions. From 009 on, an experiment
typically had:
1. a written design;
2. a confirmation set frozen before any model output on it;
3. an exploration run on exposed data only;
4. a lock;
5. a single confirmation run;
6. a report, and a closure.

The exceptions:
- **Stopped early.** 006 stopped at its quality gate and wrote no lock (006:71).
- **Discovery only.** 008 and 010 had no lock and no confirmation, by design (008:7; 010:7).
- **Never confirmed.** 020 wrote no lock, and its confirmation was never run (020:8–11).
- **Two protocol versions.** 005 ran two. A rule of its second version was amended after its first-tier results had
  been inspected (research/synthesis/source-map-005-025.md@b722e9c:264–265).
- **No exploration run.** 021–025 had no exploration phase of their own (021:34–38; 025:116).
- **Floors.**
  - In 009–012, tolerances were set from exposed Tier-A residuals (009:102–104).
  - In 013–019 the floors were frozen constants (014:15).
  - In 021–024 they were calibrated on exposed-only draws.
  - 025 had no calibration phase (025:116).
- **Reviews.**
  - From 021 on, each scientific phase ran once, with a review gate between phases (021:64–65; 022:120; 024:7–10;
    025:7–9).
  - Committed review records start at 023 (023:172–177). For 021's stated review agreement, the source map finds no
    committed record (research/synthesis/source-map-005-025.md@b722e9c:690).
  - Earlier experiments record a review or sign-off at some phases, and some record none before the confirmation
    (research/synthesis/source-map-005-025.md@b722e9c:671).

**How problems were handled.**
- **Incidents.** 020's first exploration stopped on an exact-identity check. The failure was diagnosed as a float32
  precision floor, and resolved by an authorized tolerance amendment (020:69–81).
- **Errata.** A wiring defect in 020's program was found after closure and recorded as an erratum. 021's labels stand
  as a test of that program (021:13–17).
- **Restated readings.** Some readings were narrowed or restated after closure, replacing the README wording
  (research/synthesis/source-map-005-025.md@b722e9c:636–637). Some README sections first appeared at closure
  (research/synthesis/source-map-005-025.md@b722e9c:680, 690).
- **No repeated runs.** No confirmation run was repeated. In 005's words, “a second scientific attempt requires a new
  protocol version” (005:142).

**What the locks held.**
- Each lock committed its families' criteria, their prediction rules, and every prediction that needs neither a new
  frame's reference state nor a measured input.
- Frame-conditional prediction tables were computed at the first stage of the single run, and held by digest before
  any target prompt. None was committed before scoring.
- The head read-outs apply a locked rule to the measured head input.

These are recorded in C003 (research/claims/C003-frame-conditioned-decoded-program.md@eeff9ff:130–139).

**Interventions.**
- **The E-patch** replaces the `L00.MLP` output at the cue position of a frame's reference prompt with another cue's
  encoding, `MLP₀(LN₂(W_E[w]))`. It was used from 006 to 019.
- **A real cue swap** replaces the cue token itself. It was used from 020 to 024. It “also changes three things the
  chain does not carry: the embedding itself at `p_c`; block 0's attention output at `p_c`; and, in coordinated frames,
  block 0's attention output at `p_t`.”
  (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-experiment-022-upstream-error-localization-design.md:85–86)
- **025** used a rotation of the cue's input embedding.

**Claims and evidence.**
- Results enter a claim registry graded on an evidence ladder: measurement validity, observation, localization, causal
  evidence, and beyond.
- The ladder requires that “All required gates through a maturity level must be explicitly satisfied”
  (docs/RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY.md:50).
- Without the necessity, sufficiency and control gates, or a substituted causal test with an explicit rationale, a
  claim cannot rise above localization (docs/RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY.md:83–89).

**Notation.**
- **`Y1`, `Y2`, …** are an experiment's preregistered outcome families. Their meaning differs between experiments.
  - In 009, Y1 is the head read-out.
  - In 011, Y1 is the encoding read and Y2 the read-out.
  - From 013 on, Y1 is new cues in exposed frames, and Y2 new cues in new frames.
- **The P1 read** (009) is the fixed-normalization linear `W_V W_O` read-out of the residual arriving at `L03.H04`
  (009:62–67).
- **P3** (005–007) is the isolated circuit's fidelity to the full model's contrast shift.
- **`m_T` and `m_R`** are the fractions of the transport head's and the readout's counterfactual change that the E-patch
  alone reproduces.
- **`Δx3`** is the change of the residual entering layer 3. The **E-only chain** is the program fed the encoding
  change alone.
- **The ceiling** is the frozen readout fed the measured `Δx3`. It is a comparator, “not a mathematical upper bound”
  (023:21–22).
- **Operating points.** A frame's **reference state** is the model's measured state on the frame's reference prompt.
  An **operating point** is a layer's state there. **Template bases** are reference states averaged over a family's
  exposed frames.
- **Channel D** is block 2's operating-point dependence (§4).
- **Closure fraction.** The **decoded read** is the program's prediction of what `L03.H04` reads. A **rung** is one
  neuron subset on 018's ladder. `κ` is the fraction of channel D's contribution that a subset closes.
- **`g`** is the share of the gap between the E-only chain and the ceiling that the completed program recovers
  (023:32).
  - An interpretable condition passes if `g` reaches its calibrated floor `F`, and at least 0.90.
  - It is an `ENVELOPE_ONLY_FAILURE` if it reaches 0.90 but not `F` (023:37–40).
- **018's no-harm guard** requires the locked subset's R² in every valid new frame to stay within 0.05 of the template
  base's (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-experiment-018-block2-concentration-design.md:363–365).

## 3. Localized components

**The localization (C002)** [exploratory; calibration]. The contrast is localized to three components:
- E: `L00.MLP` at the cue position, a pure function of the cue token;
- T: `L03.H04` at the prediction position, which transports the cue's number information when the cue is not the final
  token;
- R: the layer-4 and layer-5 MLPs at the prediction position.

**Its numbers.**
- On development data and nineteen held-out nouns, exact replacement of the three recovers about 98 % of the contrast
  shift, isolating them retains about 78 %, and the head alone carries about 82 % of the coordinated-template shift
  (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md@eeff9ff:148–150).
- On development data, E alone recovers 0.885, with `m_T` 0.900 and `m_R` 0.987
  (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/discovery-report-2026-09-18.md:49).

**Transport is dedicated but not exclusive.**
- `L03.H04` alone recovers 0.816 of the coordinated shift, and freezing it blocks 0.865.
- Two other heads carry small second routes: `L05.H07` alone 0.122, and `L00.H05` 0.028
  (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/discovery-report-2026-09-18.md:50–51) [exploratory].

**Replication at criterion level.** Experiment 007 recorded the same magnitudes, without outcome force, on twenty fresh
nouns in the original frames and on fresh frames
(experiments/007-supervised-cue-subspace/evidence/final-report-2026-09-18.md:109, 115):
- recovery 0.981 and 0.983;
- isolation 0.781 and 0.773, with 120/120 paired signs;
- the head alone 0.825 and 0.793.

**What the localization does not establish.**
- **Its preregistered confirmation failed its precondition.** 005's single confirmation run ended
  `BEHAVIOR_NOT_REPLICATED`: the reserve nouns failed the behavioral precondition, so no circuit family counted
  (005:125–129) [unestablished].
- **007's route did not complete.** P3's pair-level fidelity correlation failed on both fresh sets: 0.696 and 0.840
  against 0.90 (007:98–100). By 007's own rule, the route to C002's causal gates was therefore not met.
- **C002's own failed tests stand.**
  - The three templates' readout axes reach a minimum cosine of 0.292, against a floor of 0.70.
  - P3's absolute retention counts were missed on the reserve and on new frames
    (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md@eeff9ff:122–129).
  - The cue-word lexicon reached 0.699, against 0.70
    (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md@eeff9ff:50).
- **Its limitations.**
  - All splits share the same twelve prompts, so the held-out nouns test readout generalization only.
  - Necessity and isolation are scoped to pair-centered neutralization
    (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md@eeff9ff:162, 165).
- **Its scope.** It covers two cue pairs, in its three screening templates and tested replacement distributions. For
  the three new cue classes tested, `L00.MLP` alone is not the whole cue stage at the pair level, because block-0
  attention carries part of the cue's effect (§6).

C002 is registered at `LOCALIZED`; its necessity, sufficiency and control gates are open.

## 4. The decoded computation chain

The later experiments wrote programs for the computation in and around the three components. The programs' variables
are not established as the model's own (§7). In the model's order:

```text
cue token w
  → E(w): the layer-0 MLP's encoding at the cue position p_c
  → block 0 at p_c (and, in coordinated frames, at p_t): the embedding change and block-0 attention
  → layers 1–2 at p_c (and at p_t in coordinated frames): MLP corrections, the heads' value paths and pattern
    change, block 2's operating point (channel D)
  → Δx3: the change of the residual entering layer 3
  → L03.H04: transport to the prediction position p_t
  → blocks 3 and 4 at p_c and p_t, block 5 at p_t: the readout
  → Δc: the change of the singular-versus-plural contrast
```

**The stages as the records describe them.**
1. **The encoding** [exploratory].
   - For `this`, `a`, `another` and `every`, the encoding carries an on-axis number signal and an opposing off-axis part
     (010:85–89). The pattern is not general: for `these`, the rest of the encoding supports the signal (010:78–83).
   - The discovery runs behind these readings ended with `MIXED` summaries (008:65; 010:62).
   - 010's relay bound failed: layers 1–2 move the read by at least 0.29 in gross even when their net effect is near
     zero (010:64–67).
2. **Block 0.** Under a real swap, the embedding change and block-0 attention's value and pattern terms at the cue
   position add to the encoding. In coordinated frames, block 0 also updates the prediction position (023:16–20).
3. **Layers 1–2.**
   - 012's token-local MLP model, evaluated at template base states from exposed prompts (012:7–9), predicted the net
     layers-1–2 correction. It does not claim to have decoded the MLP correction itself (012:119–120).
   - 013 added the heads' value paths.
   - 015 and 016 added the heads' pattern change. 016 made it depend on the frame's reference-state channels.
   - 017 evaluated block 2's MLP at the frame's operating point. Channel D is “the frame's operating-point dependence of
     block 2's MLP as it reaches the transport read” (018:144–145).
   - A locked subset of block-2 neurons largely recovers channel D, and the frame's routing of that population is
     predicted in aggregate. This is not a uniquely defined circuit (018:156–157; 019:164–165, 186–187).
4. **Transport.** “the selective transport of number by `L03.H04` is a fixed-normalization linear read-out of
   components already present in the cue residual it receives” (009:83–85) [prospective, given the measured input].
   The head's own attention-row change is predicted from the encoding change (017).
5. **The readout.**
   - Blocks 3 and 4 run at the cue and prediction positions, and block 5 at the prediction position, with MLPs at the
     frame's operating point.
   - Block-4 attention is held at the reference, and block 5's heads keep their reference rows over the predicted value
     changes (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-22-experiment-020-readout-decompilation-design.md:106–115).
   - That includes a small second-transport path from the cue (020:43).

**The completed program.** The current end-to-end version is 023's `P1` (023:16–20).
- It is 022's committed upstream chain, the 017 chain built from the 011, 012 and 017 locks, with block 0's terms
  added. It is fed through 020's frozen readout.
- Its locked inputs are 011's axes and read, 012's template bases and 017's layer-3 bases (020:30–31). They are used
  through the frozen 012, 015, 016 and 017 programs (022:43–45).
- Block 2's MLP enters in full, at the frame's operating point, not through 018's locked subset (018:16).
- It is not a program that runs without the network: it needs each frame's measured reference state.

## 6. Real-swap prediction

These results predict the contrast change under real cue swaps, end to end.

**The readout, given the measured upstream change** (020) [exploratory].
- Fed the measured `Δx3`, the frozen readout reproduces `Δc` with R² 0.976 on exposed data. The end-to-end program,
  fed the predicted `Δx3`, reached 0.689 (020:132, 138).
- It sat below six of its twelve frozen floor conditions, and the coordinated template carried most of the shortfall
  (020:160, 177–179). That closure reading is qualified by the erratum
  (research/synthesis/source-map-005-025.md@b722e9c:638).
- These exposed values are a “calibration record, never evidence for a label” (020:110).
- 020's confirmation was never run, because its floors had been calibrated on a scratch pass with a block-5
  implementation error (020:8–11, 175–181).
- An erratum later found that 020 wired the coordinated frames' layer-1/2 reference rows through the wrong position
  (020:13–15).

**End to end on 020's wiring** (021).
- **New cues and nouns in exposed frames** [prospective]. The contrast was predicted, pooled over templates, by a small
  margin: the token-mean R² was 0.016018, against a floor clamped to 0.0 (021:116, 129–132).
- **Coordinated frames.** The prediction had no positive skill:
  - R² −0.014 in exposed frames [descriptive] (021:132–133);
  - −0.1538 in new frames, where the label was negative [unestablished] (021:134–137).
- **The erratum** qualifies all three labels. The coordinated failure “must not be read as evidence about the correctly
  wired 017 upstream chain” (021:16–17).
- **021's own caveat:** this is “not a claim of good prediction everywhere” (021:171–172).

**Where the remaining error was** (022) [prospective; Y2 conditional]. The E-only chain's remaining error was localized
“primarily to block-0 attention and the state it constructs” (022:244–245).
- Block-0 attention at the cue position accounts for 0.948 and 0.938 of the cue-final gap.
- The correctly wired layer-1–2 reductions account for at most 0.007 of either gap.
- In coordinated frames, block 0's change at the prediction position contributes positively (022:245–249).
- There is no aggregate label.

## 7. Causal tests and what remained unestablished

**Four preregistered routes to causal status were designed.** Three concerned the localized components, C002's
Level-3 gates, and one a decoded direction. Three ran, and none established causal status
(research/synthesis/synthesis-005-025.md@3d87d3c:83–87):
1. **005's confirmation run.** Its behavioral precondition failed, so none of its circuit families counted (§3).
2. **006's circuit families.** 006 stopped at its quality gate and wrote no lock, so they never ran (006:71).
3. **007's circuit families.** They ran once, on fresh data. P3's pair-level correlation failed, so 007's route to
   C002 was not met (007:98–100).
4. **025's rotation.** It rotated new cues' input embeddings along 024's nounness direction. “The official result is
   `CAUSAL_EFFECT_NOT_ESTABLISHED`” (025:244). In its own words, “It is not a finding of no causal effect.” (025:249)

Apart from 025's rotation, no preregistered, outcome-bearing test intervened on the chain's decoded variables. In C003's
words, “No preregistered, outcome-bearing necessity test in the model targeted a decoded variable.”
(research/claims/C003-frame-conditioned-decoded-program.md@eeff9ff:259–260)

**Manipulations of decoded parts outside any outcome.**
- 008's axis-only E-patches drove 0.50–0.64 of the plural cue's effect for `this`, `a` and `another`, and the whole
  encoding about none (008:57–59) [exploratory].
- At the head's output, 009 recorded mostly linear cancellation between the parts, with the non-additivity growing
  downstream (009:110–115) [exploratory].

**The boundary.** In C003's words: “The programs are prospectively predictive; their causal gates are unestablished.”
(research/claims/C003-frame-conditioned-decoded-program.md@eeff9ff:391)

**What would move it.** A preregistered test of a decoded variable itself, with necessity, sufficiency and control
arms.
- The primary test that would most change the paper acts on the component of the residual entering layer 3 at the cue
  position that `L03.H04` reads, in coordinated frames.
- It was deliberately not run before this draft
  (research/synthesis/paper-readiness-2026-09-28.md@139755d:332–348, 354–360).

## 8. Limitations and scope

- **One checkpoint** of one model, three frame families, and single-token regular nouns.
- **Small fresh sets** (research/claims/C003-frame-conditioned-decoded-program.md@eeff9ff:85–89, 409–410).
  - 23 to 40 fresh cues per experiment, drawn by quota from the tested lexical classes.
  - 6 to 18 new frames per experiment in 009–023; 024 and 025 used exposed frames only.
- **New frames are conditional.** From 013 on, new-frame results depend on the frames' measured reference states:
  “Nothing here predicts a frame's attention state from its text alone.”
  (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-experiment-016-frame-conditioned-qk-design.md:262–263)
- **How the predictions were held.** Frame-conditional prediction tables were held by digest, not committed, before
  scoring.
- **The recomputation alternative.** Much of the programs' accuracy may come from exact computation given the measured
  reference state.
  - Block 3's heads are computed by the exact layer program, and block 0 by an exact row rule. If so, the confirmations
    mainly test whether the reductions elsewhere are adequate
    (research/claims/C003-frame-conditioned-decoded-program.md@eeff9ff:331–336).
  - There is partial evidence for it at block 0: cheaper rules recovered far less than the exact rule, in cue-final
    frames (023:180–181) [descriptive].
- **Floors.** Several were calibrated against the program's own exposed performance, and some were clamped to zero
  skill (021:146–149).
- **Baselines.**
  - The record compares the programs with preregistered rival programs and with some descriptive baselines: 007's Y4
    (007:95–97), 011's fitted-rule comparison and 023's cheaper rules.
  - It has no comparison with standard interpretability pipelines for the stage programs of 012–023, or for
    end-to-end `Δc`.
- **Under a real swap, only `Δc` was scored.** The programs' reconstruction of `Δx3` there is descriptive
  (022:225–229).
- **Unresolved alternatives.** Whether the readout residual reflects rerouting or approximation error is not
  distinguished (024:374).
- **Record caveats.** The source map's record caveats apply
  ([record caveats](../synthesis/source-map-005-025.md#record-caveats)).

## 9. Relation to prior interpretability work

*This section is provisional. It will be completed after a systematic literature review.*

**Grammatical number.**
- A finalist audit, searched through 2026-09-17, lists close work:
  - inflection subspaces across models including Pythia;
  - subject–verb agreement circuits in Pythia-70M;
  - causal agreement variables across Pythia scales;
  - circuit, neuron and head accounts of agreement in other models.
- Its gap for this behavior includes that “none predicts held-out multi-component interventions for that behavior
  prospectively” (screening/behavior-candidates/audit-2026-09-17.json:30).
- The present record does not fill that gap. Its outcome-bearing prospective tests are of the E-patch, a
  single-component intervention, and of `Δc` under real swaps. Its multi-component predictions, 005's and 007's
  circuit families, did not reach outcome force.
- For C002 itself, “Confirmation was not achieved under the preregistered rule”
  (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md@eeff9ff:146).

**Methods.** The recorded neighborhood
(docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-17-experiment-005-regular-plural-mechanism-design.md:990–1003) includes:
- program extraction from small RNNs on synthetic tasks;
- compact formal guarantees for small transformers;
- solver-checkable circuit explanations for small or post-hoc-modified transformers;
- synthesized programs approximating attention heads in GPT-2 and Llama-scale models;
- self-repair after ablation, a documented confound.

**What this work may add,** as candidates to be checked against the literature:
- a long sequence of preregistered, single-run prospective tests of a mechanistic decomposition, with every failure
  kept;
- for a naturally learned behavior in a pretrained model, weight-derived, frame-conditioned stage programs. Under the
  E-patch they predicted internal states for new cues from the tested lexical classes, and in new frames for most
  stages, given the frames' reference states. The recorded boundary already includes head-level programs in pretrained
  models, so the candidate is the chain of stages tested prospectively, within the limits above;
- the explicit, registered separation of prospective prediction from causal status. By its title, the lead
  2605.22488 concerns a related distinction, between what is represented and what is computed
  (research/synthesis/paper-readiness-2026-09-28.md@139755d:197–199).

**What it does not add:**
- that number is represented;
- that agreement uses attention heads and MLPs;
- activation patching and mediation analysis;
- reading OV and QK circuits from the weights;
- programs approximating attention heads in pretrained models.

The paper-readiness assessment lists the leads the review must read
(research/synthesis/paper-readiness-2026-09-28.md@139755d:208–225).

## 10. Conclusion

- **Localized.** For two cue pairs, in C002's three screening templates and tested replacement distributions, on
  exploratory and calibration data, the contrast is localized to three components. For the new cue classes tested,
  `L00.MLP` alone is not the whole cue stage at the pair level (C002). C002's causal gates are open.
- **Predicted.** The stage programs are written from the weights, each frame's measured reference state and quantities
  locked from exposed data. By rules frozen before the target prompts were executed, they predicted:
  - the consequences of an encoding intervention, stage by stage, for new cues, and in new frames for most stages,
    given those frames' reference states;
  - the contrast change under real cue swaps, within the margins and failures reported here (C003).
- **Not established.** Whether the programs' variables are the model's causal variables. The one preregistered
  intervention on a decoded direction, 025's rotation, did not establish its effect. The three preregistered routes to
  C002's causal gates did not establish them either: C002's necessity, sufficiency and control gates are open.

The protocol that produced these results kept every failure and graded every claim. It made the boundary between
prediction and causation part of the result.
