## 1. C002 as written

- **Title:** a three-stage circuit, `L00.MLP → L03.H04 → L04/L05 MLPs`, localizes count-cued noun number selection in
  pinned Pythia-70M.
- **The hypothesis.** The singular/plural preference after a numeral or quantifier cue is carried by three components:
  - E: “the layer-0 MLP at the cue position (E, a pure token function)”
    (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:15–16);
  - T: “attention head `L03.H04` at the noun-prediction position, which transports the cue's number information when
    the cue is not the final token (T)” (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:16–18);
  - R: “the layer-4 and layer-5 MLPs at the noun-prediction position (R), which convert it into the
    singular-versus-plural logit contrast” (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:18–20).

  Replacing them moves the contrast; “holding them fixed while neutralizing every other head and MLP output retains
  most of the contrast shift” (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:21–22); and “the E → T → R chain
  mediates the effect (dedicated transport, hypothesis H1 of the design)”
  (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:22–23).
- **What the chain quantities include.** `m_T` and `m_R` were measured by patching E alone and “letting everything
  downstream recompute” (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-17-experiment-005-regular-plural-mechanism-design.md:545–546).
  So the link from E to T that C002 measured already contains whatever layers 1–2 compute from E's change.
- **What C002's own record leaves room for** (E001 on development data, exploratory, unless another source is named):
  - Isolating the three components retains 0.783 of the shift, 0.927 / 0.760 / 0.664 by template (005:96–97): the
    “Unexplained isolation residual 1 − F: 0.205”
    (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/final-report-2026-09-18.md:180). So about a fifth of the shift,
    and about a third in one template, depends on components outside the three.
  - E alone recovers 0.885 of the shift, with `m_T` 0.900 and `m_R` 0.987
    (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/discovery-report-2026-09-18.md:49). P4, E alone in the
    coordinated template, gave 0.882 on the holdout and 0.873 on the reserve
    (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:49–50).
  - E carries 0.535 of the transport-input axis, the embedding 0.009
    (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/discovery-report-2026-09-18.md:21).
  - The cue-position residual after block 0 recovers 0.965 of the coordinated shift
    (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/discovery-report-2026-09-18.md:32). The patch, A3, is defined in
    005's design (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-17-experiment-005-regular-plural-mechanism-design.md:526–530). That
    leaves about 0.035 for routes that bypass it, such as block 0's heads reading the cue from the target position.
  - Heads alone at the target position: `L03.H04` 0.816, `L05.H07` 0.122, `L00.H05` 0.028. Freezing `L03.H04` blocks
    0.865 of the cue-residual patch, `L05.H07` 0.128
    (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/discovery-report-2026-09-18.md:50–51). `L05.H07` attends 0.999
    to the cue (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/discovery-report-2026-09-18.md:40).
  - R alone recovers 0.606 (experiments/005-regular-plural-mechanism/evidence/discovery-report-2026-09-18.md:52).

  So C002 claims most of the shift, not all of it, and its own figures bound what lies outside the three components on
  its pairs.
- **The supported conclusion** (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:124), on development data and
  nineteen held-out nouns:
  - exact replacement of these components recovers about 98 % of the contrast shift;
  - isolating them retains about 78 %;
  - the head alone carries about 82 % of the coordinated-template shift.
- **The gates.**
  - Levels 0–2 are checked.
  - At Level 3, three items are checked: the intervention documented beforehand, effect sizes, and alternatives
    considered. The necessity, sufficiency and control gates are unchecked, because 005's confirmation run (E003)
    failed its behavioral precondition (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:79–86).
  - Levels 4–6 are not reached.
- **The status:** `LOCALIZED`, `ACTIVE`, last reviewed 2026-09-18, with Experiment 005 as its only related experiment
  (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:5–10).

## 4. The original claim, judged as written

**The measurements stand, and were recorded again on new nouns and new frames.**
- C002's 98 % recovery, 78 % isolation and 82 % head share are recorded measurements (E001, E002). No later
  experiment contradicts them as measured.
- 007 recorded the same magnitudes on fresh nouns in C002's frames, and on fresh frames beyond them, at criterion
  level with no outcome force (E006): recovery 0.981 / 0.983, isolation 0.781 / 0.773 with 120/120 signs, and the head

- Only P3's pair-level correlation failed (E007). Isolation keeps its mean retention (F ≈ 0.78) and the signs, but not

- C002 already attributes E003's failed retention count to the reserve split's clean flips
  (research/claims/C002-count-cued-noun-number-circuit.md:104, 136).

**The transport stage (T) is strengthened.**
- 008 located the head's selectivity in its value/output pathway, exploratorily (E009).

**Verdict on the claim as written.**
- C002's measurements stand, and were recorded again on new nouns in its own frames (E006).

- C002's own record leaves about a fifth of the shift outside the three components (isolation 0.783; §1). The later

  - block-4/5 attention in the readout.
- On C002's own pairs, C002's figures bound the named contributors:
  - E alone leaves 0.05–0.13 of the coordinated shift;

  - the routes that bypass the cue position carry about 0.035.

  The readout's downstream residual, which post-hoc analysis places in block-4/5 attention, is 0.024–0.035 of the

- Beyond the declared cue scope, at the pair level for other cue classes, block-0 attention is material (E017). C002

- Level 3 stays unestablished under rulings 1, 2 and 4.
