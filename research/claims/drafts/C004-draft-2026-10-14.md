## Hypothesis

In pinned `EleutherAI/pythia-70m-deduped`, the scalar read coordinate

    r(x) = ⟨x, w_c⟩,   w_c = γ₃ ⊙ m − mean(γ₃ ⊙ m)·𝟙,   m = W_V W_O d̂_T   (head 3.4)

of the residual `x` entering layer 3 (`RESID_PRE.L3`) at the cue position carries, in the 36 frames of Experiment
101, the `one`→`two` shift of the noun-mean contrast `c` at the prediction position, in this interventional sense:
- setting `r` in the plural (`two`) prompt to the reference (`one`) prompt's value removes the plural shift of the
  noun-mean contrast `c`;
- setting `r` in the reference prompt to the plural prompt's value produces that shift;
- equal-length edits along the cue's own centred off-read change do neither;
- doubling the read change makes the plural prompt more plural, and reversing it makes the reference prompt more
  singular.

The preregistered criteria (*Predictions*) are relative to the matched control. That removal and installation are
essentially complete is a descriptive observation of the same run.

## Scope

- **Model:** `EleutherAI/pythia-70m-deduped`, revision `e93a9faa9c77e5d09219f6c868bfc7a1bd65593c`, float32 on CPU;
  geometry and edits computed in float64 and cast to float32.
- **Site:** `RESID_PRE.L3` (`blocks.3.hook_in`) at the cue position `p_c`; the residual at the prediction position
  `p_t = p_c + 1` is not edited.
- **Frames:** the 36 frozen new frames of Experiment 101 (candidates 1–36 of its design §11), all of one coordinated
  template `NAME and NAME VERBED {cue} ADJ`; `p_c` is 4 in 10 frames, 5 in 22 and 6 in 4.
- **Population:** these 36 frames, the first 36 eligible candidates of one hand-written list, chosen by the tokenizer
  only. No population of frames, templates or cue pairs is inferred (this answers the 101 lock review's N3(a)).
- **Cues:** `one` (581) as reference and `two` (767) as plural, the pair from which the read direction was estimated;
  `three` and `many` only as a descriptive stratum.
- **Outcome:** `Δc`, the change in `c = log P(singular) − log P(plural)` at `p_t`, averaged over the 79 scorable
  **exposed** nouns that every experiment since 020 scores (the nouns are not fresh; freshness is in the frames).
- **Interventions:** exact REPLACE of `x(p_c)` at `RESID_PRE.L3` (landing and zero-outside-change checked), one forward
  pass per arm. Secondary: the head-only arms, which replace `L03.H04`'s output at `p_t` with the read edit's exact
  effect on the head while every other reader sees the unedited residual.

## Operational definitions

- `Δx = x_two − x_one` at `RESID_PRE.L3`, `p_c`; `Δr = ⟨Δx, w_c⟩`; read part `(Δr/‖w_c‖²)·w_c`; `off_c` the centred
  remainder; `û_off = off_c/‖off_c‖`; `s = |Δr|/‖w_c‖`.
- Arms: NEC-READ `x_two − read`; SUF-READ `x_one + read`; NEC-OFF `x_two − s·û_off`; SUF-OFF `x_one + s·û_off`; NEC-AMP
  `x_two + read`; SUF-NEG `x_one − read`.
- Fractions of `Δc_full = Δc(PL)`: `F_rem = Δc(NEC-READ)/Δc_full`, `F_rem_off`, `F_ins = Δc(SUF-READ)/Δc_full`,
  `F_ins_off`; `N_f = F_rem_off − F_rem`; `S_f = F_ins − F_ins_off`; `A_amp = Δc(NEC-AMP) − Δc_full`;
  `A_neg = Δc(SUF-NEG)`.
- A frame is weak when `Δc_full > −1` nat (none were).

## Predictions (preregistered before any target prompt; Experiment 101's lock and design §7)

- R: `N_f > 0` in at least 26 or 25 of 36 frames by Holm at 0.025, median `N_f ≥ 0.05`.
- I: `S_f > 0` likewise.

## Relationship to prior work

From the project's literature review (`research/paper/literature-review/review-2026-09-28.md`):
- **Agreement mechanisms:** Finlayson et al. (2021, A5) — causal mediation of number agreement in GPT-2, Transformer-XL
  and XLNet, with head results inconsistent under number swaps; Lakretz et al. (2019, A14) — number units in a two-layer
  LSTM language model; Arora, Jurafsky and Potts (2024, A7, CausalGym) — one-dimensional intervention directions for
  verb and reflexive agreement in Pythia models including 70M (DAS, probes, difference-in-means), with the caution that
  efficacy alone does not show the model uses the variable and that DAS's efficacy is not selective.
- **Causal evaluation:** causal abstraction and DAS (B1, B2); the subspace-patching illusion and its reply (B8);
  faithfulness on unmapped variables and the diagnostic value of genuinely new cues (B12) — the near-inert remainder
  addresses the first, and the in-sample axis leaves the second open.
- **Nearest difference:**
  - **The direction.** It is the read covector of a localized head: `L03.H04`'s value–output weights and layer-3
    LayerNorm gain, applied to a head-output axis `d̂_T`. That axis is a difference of means on exposed `one`/`two` and
    `each`/`several` pairs (101 design §2). So the direction is estimated on the tested pair, but it is not optimized
    against intervention outcomes, as DAS is (B2, A7).
  - **The test.** Complementary arms set the read part against the cue's own remainder, which checks the components the
    variable leaves out (B12).
  - **The prediction.** A frozen program committed before the target data predicted the fractions, close to a
    recomputation of the frozen readout (101 design §5).

## Potential novelty if confirmed

Partly new and narrow (subject to systematic comparison): a one-dimensional read of a localized head, derived from its
weights and a difference-of-means output axis estimated on the same cue pair, which carries essentially the whole tested
effect at its site while the rest of the cue's change is near-inert, shown with preregistered removal, installation,
direction and program-prediction tests. Not new: that verb and reflexive agreement have low-dimensional causal
directions in small LMs (A7).

> - **Direction (D, preregistered).** Reversing the read change made the `one` prompt more singular in 36 of 36 frames
>   (median +0.922 nats). Doubling it made the `two` prompt more plural in 28 of 36, three frames above the threshold
>   (median −0.136 nats). Both edits move the coordinate beyond the natural values the swaps use.
> - **The frozen program (P, preregistered).** Its table, committed before any target prompt, matched the measured
>   removal and installation fractions within the calibrated tolerances in 33 of 36 frames. With exact upstream inputs
>   this is close to a recomputation of the frozen readout (101 design §5). It checks the readout's fidelity under these
>   edits in noun-mean fractions, not per noun.
> - **Descriptive, with no label force.**
>   - The read part is about 14 % of the cue change's centred squared length.
>   - The rest of the cue change has a near-zero net effect: a median −0.037 of the shift in the `two` prompt.
>   - About 88–89 % of the read edit's effect passes through `L03.H04`'s output at the prediction position, and the rest
>     through other readers of the residual.
>
> For `L03.H04`'s route, the read/off-read contrast follows in part from how `w_c` is defined. The coordinate is not
> the only route: with the whole cue-position residual swapped back, a median 0.044 of the `two` shift remains. Site,
> layer, held-out-axis and baseline challenges are untested. Nothing here extends beyond these frames, this pair,
> template, site, nouns and checkpoint.

## Not supported

- That `L03.H04` alone mediates the effect (its output carries ~88–89 % in the secondary head-only arms).
- That the read coordinate is the only causal route (the closure's non-claim). With the whole `x(p_c)` swapped back, a
  median 0.044 of the `two` shift remains, and 0.152 for `many`.
- That the effect is specific to this site or layer. The same edit at `RESID_PRE.L4`, at earlier layers or at another
  position was not tested. Consumption at layer 3 rests on 005's exploratory two-frame profile and the head arms'
  88–89 % share.
- Any generalization beyond the pair, the site, the template, the nouns, the checkpoint and these frames; in particular
  to `many` (its direction family would fail) or to cue-final frames.
- That `w_c` is the unique, optimal or "true" variable, or that a simpler data-derived direction would not do as well.
- Per-noun effects of the edits: the outcome is the mean over 79 nouns, and per-noun removal and installation are not
  reported.
- That the decoded program is the model's algorithm (Level 6), or that per-noun predictions hold.
- That C002's Level-3 gates, or C003's causal gates for its programs' variables, are met (see *Relationship to C002 and
  C003*).
- Any population-level rate: the binomial tails treat these 36 frames of one template as exchangeable units under a
  sign-symmetric null.
- That this demonstrates a general method for finding causal variables in LLMs (untested; a proposed next experiment,
  `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-experiment-102-blind-read-discovery-transfer-design.md`, would test it).

## Limitations

- The axis `d̂_T` was estimated with the same `one`/`two` pair; freshness is in the frames only; nouns are exposed.
- One template, one site, one checkpoint; no site or layer negative control; a one-dimensional manipulation check.
- **The definitional component.** For `L03.H04`'s route, part of the read/off-read contrast follows from how `w_c` is
  defined. An edit orthogonal to `w_c` leaves the head's `d̂_T` output unchanged to first order: NEC-OFF 1.026 and
  SUF-OFF 0.003 of PL's ΔT. The definition does not imply three things: that the remainder is near-inert through every
  reader, that the head's output carries 88–89 %, and that the effect is complete.
- **An expected pass.** The exposed calibration already showed N, S > 0 in 36/36 frames (E101-7). The confirmation's
  value is confirmatory status on new frames, not a risky prediction borne out.
- **D extrapolates.** NEC-AMP and SUF-NEG move `r` beyond the natural values the swaps use (101 design §13; X101-1). D
  passed by 3 frames, and the record attributes the shortfall to saturation.
- Edited states are novel composites (off-distribution risk), though the swaps use the other prompt's natural `r`.
- The edits' effects on outputs other than `c` (the rest of the next-token distribution) are not reported.
- One confirmation run, no replication; every review is an AI review; no external human expert has reviewed it.
